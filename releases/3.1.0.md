## What this release is about

This release is mostly other people's work, and I'm very grateful for it. Andrew Turbay ([@turbay-a](https://github.com/turbay-a)) brought **Microsoft Teams and Mattermost** as channels, **ratings asked in the chat itself**, and **Proxmox VE and VMware Cloud Director** servers alongside vCenter. Santhosh Srinivasan (Sandy, [@srinivasansanthosh](https://github.com/srinivasansanthosh)) brought **automatic asset tags**, **labels you design** and recognition of a **renamed computer by its serial number**. Alongside those: **cost centres** that stay in step with your finance system, **suppliers and their contacts in People**, and the inbox now **refreshes itself** when anything new arrives. Nothing breaks, and everything that changes how FreeITSM behaves is **off after upgrading** until you switch it on.

## Security

- **Some command-line tools could be run from a browser.** In every release from 1.9.0 to 3.0.0, 13 of the tools in the `scripts` folder (mostly the translation tools) ran for anyone who requested them, without signing in. No data could be read or changed: most of them stopped at once, though where PHP is set to display errors, the error showed the server's folder paths. One rewrote the self-service portal's background images with identical copies. Every one of them now refuses unless run from the command line, and the folder is closed on Apache, IIS and the supplied nginx configuration. Nothing to do beyond upgrading; if you run nginx with your own configuration, block `.php` files under `/scripts/`, as the supplied one now does.

## New features

- **Microsoft Teams channel.** People in your organisation chat to a service desk bot in Teams, one to one, and each conversation becomes a ticket, like email, WhatsApp and Telegram. Only people in your own Microsoft 365 tenant can use it, and the bot needs no Microsoft Graph permissions. **Tickets → Settings → Messaging**, with a guide: [Microsoft Teams](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Microsoft-Teams). Contributed by Andrew Turbay ([PR #166](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/pull/166)).
- **Mattermost channel.** A support channel on your Mattermost server, with replies in the thread. Guide: [Mattermost](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Mattermost). Also from PR #166.
- **Teams and Mattermost know who people are.** Like Slack, a person whose email address FreeITSM already has gets their chat tickets filed under them, with their other tickets and their company, rather than as a new contact. On Mattermost, only an email address Mattermost has verified is trusted. Andrew's suggestion.
- **Ratings asked in the chat.** A customer who wrote in on WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Teams or Mattermost can be asked for their satisfaction rating in that chat instead of by email: buttons on Telegram, Teams and Mattermost, a reply of 1 to 5 elsewhere. On Telegram the buttons are replaced by their answer once they tap one. **Tickets → Settings → CSAT → Ask chat customers in their chat**, off after upgrading.
- **Proxmox VE and VMware Cloud Director servers.** Their virtual machines, with IP addresses, network cards and disks, appear on **Asset Management → Servers** next to vCenter, with a **Source** column. You can add several servers of each, under **Asset Management → Settings**. FreeITSM only reads from them, and passwords and API tokens are stored encrypted. A virtual machine is only removed when its server has genuinely stopped listing it, never because a node or a page of the list failed to load. Guides: [Proxmox VE](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Proxmox-VE) and [VMware Cloud Director](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/VMware-Cloud-Director). Contributed by Andrew Turbay ([PR #167](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/pull/167)).
- **Cost centres**, under **System → Cost Centres**. Keep each company's list of cost codes, with codes kept exactly as written (`0010` stays `0010`) and parent codes for a hierarchy. Import and export Excel or CSV, with a preview first, or let your finance system keep the list in step through the REST API. This is stage one of [#160](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/160), asked for by bigtrodat: charging assets to a cost centre comes next.
- **Automatic asset tags.** New assets are numbered for you in a format you choose (`AST-00001`, or with the company or year in it), per company or across the whole install, and a number is never used twice. **Asset Management → Settings → Asset tags**, off until you switch it on. Contributed by Sandy ([PR #164](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/pull/164)).
- **Labels you design.** Choose which details a printed asset label shows and in what order, including your own custom fields, and add a header, footer and your logo in the middle of the QR code. **Asset Management → Settings → Asset labels & QR**. From Sandy's PR #164.
- **A renamed computer is still the same asset.** The inventory agent and Intune now recognise a computer by its serial number before its name, so a rename updates the asset instead of creating a second one. A replacement laptop given the old one's name becomes a new asset, and its history says which old asset still carries the name. From Sandy's PR #164.
- **Suppliers in People.** **People → Suppliers** has a page for each supplier and each supplier contact, showing their contracts, the equipment bought from them and the domains they're involved in. From [#153](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/153), asked for by mbsouth.
- **More choice on a domain.** Its technical contact can be one of your own analysts as well as a supplier contact, and its customer can be a supplier as well as a person. From [#162](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/162), also asked for by mbsouth.
- **Photo Album**, under **System**, just for fun: your webcam picture drawn live in text characters, to save, copy or download. The photo never leaves your browser.

## Improvements

- **The inbox keeps itself up to date.** Every minute it checks for anything new on your tickets, including an email collected in the background, a chat message or a colleague's reply, and refreshes the list when something has arrived. Until now a WhatsApp or Telegram message sat unseen until someone pressed refresh.
- **Intune keeps a device linked to its asset** after you move the asset to another company, and **Asset Management → Settings → Intune** now chooses which company new devices go into, and whether an asset is renamed when its device is renamed in Intune (off after upgrading).
- **Cost centres follow the company switcher**, including **All companies**, with a Company column.
- **The help pages, the wiki and Feature Bingo** cover everything in this release.

## Fixes

- **Tickets opened from a chat ignored your ticket number format.** With numbering set to something like `TICKET-{######}`, tickets from WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack and web chat, approved forms, merges, splits and workflows still got old-style numbers such as `JNL-471-47705`. New tickets now all follow your format; existing ones keep their numbers.
- **On a ticket with more than seven messages, the newest were hidden** behind the "older messages" line, and the oldest were left on show. The newest now stay at the top. This had been the case since 1.0.0.
- **A ticket reopened by a customer's reply still counted as closed** in reports.
- **Saving a domain could clear its technical contact** if the analyst had no access to Contracts.
- **A domain's history showed "customer_user_id"** instead of "Customer".

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**. It creates the tables for Proxmox and Cloud Director servers, cost centres and asset tags, and adds the columns for ratings in the chat and domain contacts.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5).

**If you use Telegram:** open your Telegram channel in **Tickets → Settings → Messaging** and click **Connect** once. Until you do, rating buttons in Telegram won't respond. **Test** on the channel tells you whether it's needed.

**New permissions:** Proxmox VE servers, VMware Cloud Director servers, Asset tags, Asset labels & QR and Discovery & reconciliation, under **System → Roles**. Administrators have them already. The REST API has a new **cost_centres** permission for API keys.

**Optional scheduled tasks:** to sync Proxmox and Cloud Director on a schedule, run `php cron/proxmox_sync.php` and `php cron/vcloud_sync.php` every few minutes. The **Sync hypervisors** button on the Servers page works without them.

The new screens in this release are in English only for now, and the other languages will follow.

## Rollback

Safe - this release only adds to the database, so you can go back to 3.0.0. Tickets numbered in your own format while on 3.1.0 keep those numbers, and 3.0.0 still finds them.
