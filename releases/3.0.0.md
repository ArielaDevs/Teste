## What this release is about

**Nothing breaks.** By the usual rule this would be 2.11.0: it only adds, your configuration keeps working, and you can roll back. I chose 3.0.0 because it adds whole new modules, and because Report Packs brings a powerful new kind of design code to FreeITSM - a step change in what it can do. **Report Packs** is a Word-like designer for multi-page reports that export to PDF, modelled on the monthly service report of a supporter of the project. A new **People** module gives one page per person and per company, drawn from every module at once, and contracts can now be with a **customer** as well as a supplier, both from mbsouth's request. The LMS gains **competency tests** for checking a candidate's skills before an interview. Domains now link to the CMDB, Service Status, tickets, knowledge articles and contracts. The release also protects every screen against cross-site request forgery and closes three gaps where information could reach someone who should not see it, so **upgrade if you run more than one company**.

## Security

- **Protection against cross-site request forgery, everywhere.** Until now, a malicious web page visited by a signed-in analyst or portal user could make their browser change things in FreeITSM on their behalf. Every change now has to come from FreeITSM's own pages and carry a token that only those pages have. Nothing needs setting up; the REST API, inventory agents, messaging webhooks and the web chat widget work as before. **After upgrading, reload any FreeITSM tabs that were already open**, or their next save will be refused.
- **Assets → Users showed people from other companies.** In every release from 1.0.0 to 2.10.2, an analyst limited to some companies could read the email address, phone numbers, job title, employee number and manager of a person in any company, by changing the number in the address. They now see only people in their own companies, and the same applies to a manager's list of reports.
- **Confidential tickets could still leave by two routes.** In 2.8.0 to 2.10.2, a workflow's *Send email* step could send a confidential ticket to any address it named, and scheduled confidential tickets appeared in Outlook, CalDAV and the calendar feed with their subject and requester. *Send email* now only reaches the requester or analysts, and calendars show just "Confidential ticket".
- **Three actions could be triggered by a plain link**: starting an RFP Builder AI generation, deleting a checklist template and deleting a problem. They now need a real request from FreeITSM's own pages.

## New features

- **Report Packs.** Asked for by a supporter of the project, under **Reporting → Report Packs**. Design a multi-page report the way you would in Word: a ribbon, real pages with rulers, and a searchable toolbox of blocks to drag on. The blocks cover tickets (by status, priority, category, analyst, team and more, trends over time, a ticket list), service uptime with daily bars, the incident log, software, assets and Intune. Add headings, text, a cover page, a contents page, and a header and footer with your logo, page numbers and the report's dates. Choose the period and company once and every block follows it. Export to PDF in one click, and the PDF matches the screen exactly. Packs are private until you share them with an analyst, a team or a department, as **View** or **Edit**. Everyone who opens a shared pack sees the figures their own access allows, never the author's.
- **People.** A new module with one page per person and one per company, showing their tickets, the equipment they hold, contracts and domains they are the customer for, courses and form submissions together. **People → Companies** shows each company as a card with its people, open tickets and equipment, with a search box. From [#153](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/153), asked for by mbsouth.
- **Contracts with customers.** A contract can now be with a customer you sell to, as well as a supplier you buy from. Choose **Supplier** or **Customer** on the contract, and a customer can be a company, a person, or both. A domain can also name the customer it is for. From [#153](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/153) and [#154](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/154).
- **Competency tests**, under **LMS → Tests**. Describe the role you are recruiting for and add the skills it needs, each with a difficulty, a number of questions and a format: multiple choice, or *graded*, where four reasonable answers are worth different marks in the style of the ITIL exams. The AI drafts the questions into a question bank, and nothing reaches a candidate until you have approved it. You can search the bank, edit or hide questions, and build a test from it by hand. Each candidate gets a private, single-use link with a timer and no account needed, and every candidate sits an identical paper. Results show a score for each skill and every answer they chose. Candidates' details are deleted after 180 days unless you choose otherwise.
- **Domains joined to the rest of FreeITSM.** A domain's new **Connections** tab links it to CMDB items, tickets and knowledge articles, and shows its contract. Each link shows on both sides, so a ticket shows its domains and a CI shows the domains it depends on. Link a domain to the Service Status services that run on it, and when the domain or its certificate expires, FreeITSM can flag those services or raise the incident itself (**Domains → Settings → Service Status**). Certificate renewals now appear in the Calendar too, and you can right-click a domain in the register for everything you can do to it.
- **Full screen editing in the Knowledge base.** A **Full screen** button beside **Save** hides everything but the article editor.
- **A check of your config.php.** A new debug tool, **Config completeness**, compares your `config.php` with the settings this version expects and gives the line to add for anything missing. It never shows a value.

## Improvements

- **Domains, People and Assets → Users work properly on a phone.**
- **FreeITSM no longer depends on one block in config.php to find its own address.** A config.php missing it keeps working instead of failing on every page.
- **The Reporting home page** shows its four areas as even cards instead of squeezing them into a narrow box.
- **The help pages and Feature Bingo are up to date** with everything in this release, including new guides for People, Domains connections, Report Packs and competency tests.

## Fixes

- **Adding a relationship type in the CMDB always failed** with an SQL error. Reported by rocstat1979 ([#163](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/163)).
- **Times were wrong in reply emails.** The "On ... wrote:" lines in a reply's quoted thread showed the time in UTC rather than local time, and the Docker image ignored the timezone you set. Reported by An Duong ([#161](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/161)).
- **Emails collected from a Gmail mailbox showed the wrong time received**, ahead by the server's difference from UTC. Emails already collected keep their old times.
- **A CI's ticket list showed every ticket as a number with an unknown status.**
- **Dates showed as a dash, or in the server's time**, on Assets → Users, a CMDB item's page and the knowledge assistant.
- **A person or company page taller than the window could not be scrolled.**

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**. It creates the tables for Report Packs, competency tests and Domains connections, and adds the columns for contract and domain customers.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5), and **reload any other FreeITSM tabs** that were open during the upgrade.

Competency tests have their own permission, **Competency tests**, because they hold the details of people outside your organisation. Administrators have it already; give it to anyone else who recruits through a role in **System → Roles**. Writing questions with AI uses the provider set in **LMS → Settings → LMS AI**.

On Docker, set the `TZ` environment variable if you want FreeITSM's default timezone to be something other than UTC.

The new screens in this release are in English only for now, and the other languages will follow.

## Rollback

Safe - this release only adds to the database, so you can go back to 2.10.2. Code from before this release does not send the cross-site request forgery token, which is fine, because it does not check for one either.
