## What this release is about

A big one, and much of it came from other people. Andrew Turbay contributed a **Telegram** channel, and Santhosh Srinivasan (Sandy) contributed finer control over who single sign-on creates accounts for, and what it keeps in step. Several things people asked for on GitHub are in here too: portal sign-in by email domain, filters on the CSAT page, and replies from SMTP and Gmail mailboxes that finally carry their attachments. On top of that, tasks gain checklists and a much more useful board, the bell can show its notifications on your desktop, and on a phone a long press on a ticket now gives you everything the right-click menu does. It also closes a security hole in ticket checklists, so **if you run more than one company, upgrade**.

## Security

- **Checklists on tickets were not limited to the analyst's companies.** In 2.0.0 to 2.9.0, any signed-in analyst who knew a ticket's number could read, attach, tick off and remove the checklists on that ticket - including a ticket in a company they cannot otherwise see, and even without access to the Tickets module. Every checklist action on a ticket now checks both, and answers "not found" for a ticket the analyst cannot reach. If you run more than one company, or give some analysts no Tickets access, upgrade.

## New features

- **Telegram channel.** Contributed by Andrew Turbay ([PR #159](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/pull/159)). Create a bot, paste its token into **Tickets → Settings → Messaging**, press **Connect**, and messages to the bot become tickets you answer from the reading pane. There is no 24-hour reply window, and the bot writes in each person's own Telegram language. The first time someone writes, the bot asks them once to share their phone number, and if that matches one person in the company the conversation is linked to them.
- **Send pictures and documents from the chat reply box**, on Telegram and on WhatsApp (Meta and Twilio). Files are checked like any attachment and arrive with their real name.
- **Single sign-on: separate rules for requesters and analysts.** Contributed by Sandy ([discussion #155](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/discussions/155)). Each sign-in provider now has its own switches for creating accounts for portal requesters and for IT analysts. It also chooses what happens when someone without an analyst account signs in on the analyst login: warn and ask, send them to the portal, or refuse. It can keep each person's name, job title, department, office and phone numbers in step with your identity provider, either once when the account is made or at every sign-in. Anyone with both kinds of account gets an **Analyst console** link in the portal's account menu.
- **Portal sign-in by email domain.** Asked for in [#147](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/147). Give a sign-in provider your email domains, and anyone who types an address on one of them in the portal goes straight to that provider, even on their first visit. Hide the provider's button as well and the portal sign-in page can be just an email box and **Continue**, for staff and customers alike.
- **Filters on the CSAT page.** Asked for in [#157](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/157). Pick any From and To dates, a rating band (positive, neutral or negative), an analyst or a customer. The responses are now paged and show which customer left each one. On a phone the filters sit behind a **Filters** button.
- **Checklists on tasks.** Asked for by Sandy in [discussion #138](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/discussions/138), and off until you switch it on in **Tasks → Settings → Checklists**. Tasks and subtasks can then carry checklists from the Checklists module, with the same Standard and Critical rules as tickets. A task can have several checklists, and you can drag the checklists, and the steps inside them, into whatever order you like.
- **A better task board.**
  - **Columns by analyst.** A new **Columns** switch shows one column per person instead of one per status. Drag a card to someone else's column to give them the task.
  - **Subtasks under their card**, drawn like the branches of a tree, each one a click away. A personal setting hides them once they are all done.
  - **Inside a task**, you can drag subtasks into order by a handle, and their start and due dates sit in tidy columns you can change in place.
  - **The large window's tabs** look like real tabs, all the same width, and each shows how many things it holds, Documents included.
- **Desktop notifications.** Turn them on in **Preferences → Notifications** and new items in the bell also pop up on your computer (in Edge on Windows, as ordinary Windows notifications) while FreeITSM is open in a tab.
- **An email when a task is assigned to you**, if you ask for one in **Preferences → Notifications**. It is written in your own language, and nothing is sent for a task you assign to yourself.
- **The ticket menu on a phone.** Press and hold a ticket and the full right-click menu opens as a full-screen panel. Choices with more options, such as **Set priority**, slide across to them.

## Improvements

- **The reply window gives the message more room.** The editor is taller, To and Cc have their labels beside the boxes, and Templates and Signature have moved to the bottom left next to **Send**.
- **System → Single Sign-On shows both sign-out addresses** to register with your identity provider, each with **Copy**. Previously the Keycloak guide named only one of them, so signing out of the portal failed.
- **Unlink a person from their sign-in provider** in **Tickets → Users**, for someone who was linked to the wrong account or has moved to another provider.
- **Emails sent through an SMTP mailbox are less likely to be marked as spam.** They now carry the date and message identity headers that the email standard requires.
- **A screenshot pasted into a reply now shows in the customer's email.** It used to be sent in a form Gmail and Outlook will not display.
- **The help pages are up to date** with everything in this release, including a new section on the notification bell.

## Fixes

- **Replies and forwards from an SMTP or Gmail mailbox arrived without their attachments**, while the ticket showed them as sent. Reported by An Duong ([#158](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/158)), with mbsouth spotting the same on forwards. Gmail replies also lost their Cc list, and pictures in the quoted part of a reply were broken on every type of mailbox. All fixed.
- **On a multi-company install, global sign-in providers were missing from the portal sign-in page.** Reported by An Duong ([#156](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/156)).
- **The CSAT page showed every company's results** whichever company was picked in the header. It now follows the company switcher like the ticket list ([#157](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/157)).
- **System → Companies and Tickets → Triage could not be scrolled**, so anything below the bottom of the window was out of reach.
- **On an install in a subfolder**, saving a note from a ticket's checklist and asking for a checklist suggestion did not work.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**. It creates the tables for checklists on tasks and adds new columns for single sign-on and the portal. Your existing sign-in providers keep behaving exactly as before.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5). This release updates cached JavaScript and styles.

The chat reply box is translated into 13 languages. Most other new screens in this release are in English only for now, and the other languages will follow.

To use Telegram, FreeITSM needs an address on the internet that Telegram can reach over https. Set it as the **Public base URL** in **Tickets → Settings → Messaging**, as WhatsApp already uses.

## Rollback

Safe - this release only adds to the database, so you can go back to 2.9.0.
